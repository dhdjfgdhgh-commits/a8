About Banquet

✦

For hosts who love a long table.

Banquet is an independent site about hosting generous dinners: family celebrations, holiday tables and gatherings of many.

We focus on the parts that make big dinners feel effortless: a well-set table, a thoughtful seating plan and a menu timed so every course arrives at its best. Our tools do the arithmetic; our guides share the craft.

Independent
We do not sell tableware, food or event services.
Practical
Advice tested at real tables, from eight guests to many more.
Reviewed
Every guide is checked by a second editor and dated.
